## Information Notice on the Processing of Personal Data

In line with Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), **{controller}**, as the data controller, informs you about how your personal data is processed.

**Data we process.** Name, contact details (e-mail, phone), job title, employer and career history, city, LinkedIn profile address, notes on our conversations with you and information about application processes.

**Purposes.** Providing executive search and recruitment services, contacting you, assessing suitable roles and sharing your profile with our clients (only if you gave explicit consent), and meeting legal obligations.

**Legal bases.** Processing that is necessary to establish, exercise or protect a legal right, and the legitimate interest of the controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-e and 5/2-f); and your explicit consent where it applies (Art. 5/1). Explicit consent is asked for separately, in its own step; declining it does not affect your legal rights.

**Transfers.** We do not share your data with third parties without your explicit consent. We may disclose it to competent public authorities when the law requires. Transfers abroad only take place with the safeguards of KVKK Art. 9.

**Retention.** By default we keep your data for 2 years from the last contact or the date it was recorded, then delete or anonymise it. If you renew your consent, the period starts again.

**Your rights (KVKK Art. 11).** You may ask whether your data is processed, request information, ask for correction or deletion, object to the processing and claim compensation for damage. Send your request to us by e-mail; we answer within 30 days.
