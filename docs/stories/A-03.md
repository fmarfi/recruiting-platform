# A-03 - Contacts per account

**Priority:** Must  
**Requirement:** A-03 in `docs/requirements.md`  
**Status:** Proposed (until the client validates)

## User story

As a consultant, I want contacts under each account with their role and contact details, linked to the network, so that I know who to talk to at a client.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Add a contact**
   - Given an existing account
   - When the user adds a contact with name, role title, e-mail and phone
   - Then the contact appears on the account's contact tab and also as a person in the network (A-12) of type CLIENT_CONTACT
2. **Reuse an existing person**
   - Given a person already in the network
   - When the user adds them as a contact of an account
   - Then no duplicate person is created; the person now shows the account in their company history
3. **E-mail format**
   - Given the contact form
   - When the e-mail is not a valid address
   - Then the form is not saved and the e-mail field shows an error
4. **Remove a contact**
   - Given an account contact
   - When the user chooses "End contact"
   - Then the link gets an end date (`endedOn`), the contact is hidden from the contact list by default (a filter shows ended contacts), the ended role stays in the account's company history, and the person stays in the network

## Dependencies

A-01, A-12
