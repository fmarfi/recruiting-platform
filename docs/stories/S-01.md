# S-01 - KVKK notice and consent e-mail

**Priority:** Must  
**Requirement:** S-01 in `docs/requirements.md`  
**Status:** Proposed (until the client validates)

## User story

As the firm (data controller), I want an information notice and consent request e-mailed automatically when a person's data enters the system, with tracked status, so that we meet KVKK from day one.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Notice sent automatically**
   - Given a new person of type CANDIDATE with an e-mail address
   - When they are saved
   - Then a notice and consent e-mail in the person's language is queued and sent, and status becomes SENT
2. **Consent given**
   - Given a SENT request
   - When the person opens the link and confirms
   - Then status becomes GIVEN with text version, timestamp and channel stored (NFR-02)
3. **Refused**
   - Given a SENT request
   - When the person refuses
   - Then status becomes REFUSED and the person is flagged on pipelines pending review
4. **Withdrawn**
   - Given a GIVEN consent
   - When the person withdraws via the link
   - Then status becomes WITHDRAWN with the withdrawal date, and the history keeps all prior states
5. **No e-mail address**
   - Given a candidate without e-mail
   - When they are saved
   - Then status stays NOT_SENT and a task shows "collect consent another way"
6. **Reminder**
   - Given a request SENT 7 days ago with no answer
   - When the daily job runs
   - Then one reminder is sent; no further reminders
7. **Information notice for all**
   - Given a person added from a LinkedIn source
   - When they are saved
   - Then they also get the information notice (NFR-01); explicit consent is a separate step

## Dependencies

A-12, e-mail infrastructure (Mailpit in dev)

## Open questions

Legal wording and versions (KVKK lawyer). Does a notice go to client contacts and referrers too (assumed yes for all stored persons)? Reminder timing.

## Notes

Consent statuses (closed set, from the demo): NOT_SENT, SENT, GIVEN, REFUSED, WITHDRAWN. ASSUMPTION on allowed moves: NOT_SENT to SENT; SENT to GIVEN or REFUSED; GIVEN to WITHDRAWN; REFUSED to GIVEN (person changes mind); WITHDRAWN to SENT (new request).
